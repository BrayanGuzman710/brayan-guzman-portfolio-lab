Hello, welcome to my Engineering Portfolio my name is Brayan Maldonado-Guzman. I am a Mechanical Engineering student at UNC Charlotte.

I have always liked understanding how things work and finding how to make them better. I think this is what made me want to be in engineering. I like to be hands on and working on problems. I have completed an apprenticeship in Mechatronics and got a associates degree with that. I also got a journeyman's license. I currently work for Dauch Corporation as a maintenance technician. Working there has gave me hands on experience in a real manufacturing plant. Troubleshooting equipment and working with automated systems has showed me what the job standard is in a manufacturing plant.

What I enjoy most about engineering is that there will always be room for growth. There will always be something new to learn or work on. As I move on with this degree I hope to further my skills in depth and hopefully one day design something that can help the world as a whole.
