One Design Rule: 
Wall thickness is very important in additive manufacturing. In 3D printing wall thickness refers to the thickness of outer surfaces or shells. When wall thickness is incorrect it can affect your part negatively. This can lead to weak parts, gaps, and deformation.     

Source: https://bigrep.com/posts/designing-wall-thickness-for-3d-printing/  

Specific Consideration:
Warping is a consideration. Warping is when a material cools unevenly and the corners or edges begin to lift off the print bed. This can be avoided by avoiding large flat areas when possible and adding rounded corners. They can also use things like a brim to help the part stay attached. 

Source: https://help.prusa3d.com/article/warping_2011

Fdam: Something I learned from my teammates was the 45 degree rule. It is mostly about overhangs around 45 degrees. They can usually be printed without supports. This is important because you can save material and shorten print time. Fdm: My group talked about infill, which is the internal structure printed inside a part. A designer can adjust the infill percentage and pattern to give the part enough strength while reducing its weight, material use, and printing time.
